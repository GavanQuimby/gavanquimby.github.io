Internship · May – Aug 2025

IT Intern at Settlers Hospitality

Day-to-day IT for a hospitality group: hotels, restaurants, payment systems, access control, cameras, and point of sale. Half the job was field work, running cable, mounting access points, and standing up new locations. The other half was supporting the people who depend on all of it.

What I worked on

Four kinds of work filled the summer, from network hardware on the roofline to the menu buttons on a server's handheld.

Network

Hotel network modernization

Helped replace legacy TP-Link hardware and deploy a dedicated television network that improved wireless performance across the property.

  • Mapped hardware and network equipment across properties.
  • Mounted indoor and outdoor wireless access points.
  • Cleaned up network closets, racks, and cabling.
Launch

Opening Ale Mary's at the Bittenbender

Configured POS workstations, handheld devices, printers, and network equipment for a new restaurant against a hard launch deadline.

  • Programmed menu items in the Oracle MICROS Enterprise Management Console.
  • Set up payment terminals and handhelds for service.
  • Validated the setup with staff before opening day.
Security

Cameras and access control

Installed and configured security cameras and smart locks across several properties in line with Pennsylvania surveillance compliance requirements.

  • Ran structured and low-voltage cabling to new devices.
  • Supported staff access workflows for property operations.
Endpoints

Imaging and malware recovery

Imaged and deployed Windows laptops for new hires and hardware replacements, and rebuilt machines after malware infections.

  • Handled tickets for hardware, software, accounts, and connectivity.
  • Supported Microsoft 365 users across hotels and restaurants.

In the field

Microsoft 365, Windows imaging, Oracle MICROS Simphony, payment terminals, access points, cameras, smart locks, switches and racks, and a lot of cable.
